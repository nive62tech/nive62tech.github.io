About Me

Building agentic AI
that ships

I'm a final-year AI & ML engineering student specializing in Generative AI and Agentic AI Engineering — building systems that don't just answer a prompt, but plan, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks autonomously. I work with RAG pipelines, LangChain, LangGraph, and multi-agent architectures, on projects that go beyond typical tutorials — like an agent that debugs and fixes its own failing code, and a system where AI agents attack and defend each other to stress-test security.

Alongside the research, I make sure everything I build can actually reach production — FastAPI, Docker, and applied MLOps — so I'm not dependent on someone else to ship my work. I also serve as Founding Chair of my IEEE student chapter, which taught me a lot about execution and ownership beyond just code.
